## Problem Statement

Predicting the crop yield is quite challenging as agricultural produce depends on multiple factors like the soil, rainfall, humidity, temperature, use of fertilizers, and the crop. There is a need for an easy way to predict the expected crop yield and compare it to the actual yield after harvesting.

## Scope of the Project

The project is about prediction of the crop yield from the field condition entered by the user. It will also enable the user to enter actual field conditions as well as actual yield after harvesting, compare them, find the difference between them, and present it graphically.

## Target Users

* Farmers
* Students and researchers in Agriculture
* Agricultural professionals
* Farm Managers
* Any other individual interested in crop yield prediction

## High-Level Features

* Entry of field conditions for prediction of yield.
* Prediction of the crop yield.
* Entry of the actual field conditions after harvesting.
* Recording of the actual crop yield.
* Comparison of predicted and actual crop yield.
* Calculation of the difference between yields.
* Presentation of results graphically.
